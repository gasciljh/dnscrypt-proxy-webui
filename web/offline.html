💡 How to restart the service?

  • Open Magisk Manager or KernelSU.
  • Go to Modules → DNSCrypt Proxy.
  • Tap the Action button to restart.
  • Or from terminal:
    su -c "sh /data/adb/modules/dnscrypt-proxy-webui/action.sh --restart"

🚨 If the module is unbootable

  • Create the recovery trigger file:
    su -c "touch /data/adb/modules/dnscrypt-proxy-webui/recovery"
  • Reboot the device:
    su -c "reboot"
  • On next boot, the installer will restore the last known-good configuration from /sdcard/dnscrypt-webui-backup/.
  • Full guide: docs/EMERGENCY.md
